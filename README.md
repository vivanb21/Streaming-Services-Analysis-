# Streaming-Services-Analysis-
Streaming Services Market Analysis
  A SQL-based analysis of major streaming service platforms, exploring pricing strategy, subscriber trends, revenue potential, and churn risk.
Objective
   To analyze the competitive landscape of streaming services and surface actionable insights around revenue performance, customer retention, and pricing efficiency.
Questions Explored

Which services generate the most monthly and annual revenue?
  How does churn rate correlate with revenue loss?
  What is each service's market share by subscriber count?
  How many months does it take a service to break even on its annual plan vs. monthly?
  What is the revenue impact of offering discounted annual pricing?

Tools
 MySQL

Key Outputs
  Revenue comparison across monthly and annual pricing models
  Churn-based revenue loss estimates per service
  Market share breakdown by subscriber base
  Retention risk categorization (Market Leader / Stable / High Risk)
  Consolidated SQL View (stream_services_vis) aggregating all key metrics for visualization
